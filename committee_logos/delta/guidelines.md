# Brand Guidelines for Logo: > DELTΔ\_

## 1. Structure and Anatomy

At its core, the logo is entirely text-based and must be constructed precisely using the following sequence of characters:

1. `>` (Greater-than sign)
2. ` ` (Space)
3. `D`
4. `E`
5. `L`
6. `T`
7. `Δ` (Greek capital letter delta)
8. `_` (Underscore / Low line)

Since the logo is constructed entirely from standardized characters, it possesses a technical flexibility that allows it to be rendered in different typefaces and environments without losing its semantic structure.

## 2. Typography and Color Specification

To ensure a consistent visual identity, the following primary parameters must be applied as far as technically possible:

- **Primary Typeface:** _Noto Sans_ (or _Noto Sans Mono_ if a monospaced font is required to reinforce the code aesthetic).
- **Font Weight:** The logo must always be rendered in its entirety in bold (**Bold** / Font weight 700) to ensure visual weight and high legibility.
- **Color Coding:** Alphanumeric characters (`D`, `E`, `L`, `T`) and the subsequent space are rendered in the base color (black or white depending on the background).
- **Special Characters:** The characters `>`, `Δ`, and `_` must be colored using the section's specific profile color: **Råsa**.
  - **sRGB (HEX):** `#F280A1`

## 3. Alternative Execution (Symbol)

In contexts where the full logo text is inappropriate or where space is limited (such as a favicon or profile picture in digital systems), the single delta character may be used as a standalone symbol:

- **Δ** (Greek capital letter delta)

Even in this standalone execution, the _Noto Sans_ typeface, bold weight (**Bold**), and the section color _Råsa_ (`#F280A1`) must be applied preferentially.
